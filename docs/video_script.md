# Video script (≤ 2 minutes) — v4, built-in ML, no AI keys

Record with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or Cmd+Shift+5.
Before recording: load the extension (`chrome://extensions` → Load unpacked → `extension/`), open an Amazon product page
(e.g. headphones or running shoes), click **Lock** in the side panel so the password screen is showing.
Speak in English; times are a guide.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Amazon product page | "Families decide about money one purchase at a time — in a shop, often a teen with a phone. Budget apps show what already happened. FinPilot decides *before* you click Buy." |
| 0:12–0:24 | Side panel: password screen → type **DEMO26** → Open | "FinPilot lives inside Chrome. The whole family opens it with one password — mom, dad or a teen. No server and no AI keys: our own ML models run right in the browser." |
| 0:24–0:46 | In the panel flip **Shop check** on → the card appears on the product page | "On any product page FinPilot gives the answer first: Not worth it. Then why, in the family's own numbers: $322 left for shopping this month, and our model says the chance of overspending jumps to 96%. And the average market price of similar products from our database of 66,000 Amazon products." Click **Postpone**. |
| 0:46–1:02 | Advisor: advice cards, Left to spend | "The Advisor shows what is left in each category and the ML risk of going over — groceries are at 88%, about $3 a day left. Bills due this week were found by another model from the bank history." |
| 1:02–1:14 | Advisor → Is it worth it? → "Concert tickets", 100 | "Concert tickets are fine — at $100 they're not. A price that fits this family right now is about $16." |
| 1:14–1:28 | Money: type "Chipotle burrito bowl", 18.50 → ML picks Dining → Add; **Connect PayPal → Log in with PayPal** (sandbox personal account) → payments appear | "The family writes expenses and income here; the model picks the category as you type.
